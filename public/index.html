How the pool pays

All fees belong to holders. The pool pays out a steady share each day, so rewards keep flowing even when trading goes quiet.

100%of trading fees go to the holders' pool
20%of the pool paid out per day, a slice every 10 min
0 SOLprice per pixel

Every 10 minutes a small slice of the pool, adding up to 20% a day, is split between holders by their average number of live pixels over those 10 minutes. The rest stays in the pool as a cushion: if trading slows down, payouts carry on from what has built up. A pixel painted over stops counting the moment it is taken.

each 10 min: pool × 0.155% × your avg. live pixels ÷ all avg. live pixels (= 20% of the pool a day)

Questions

Still unsure? Paste any wallet below and see what it earns.

Do I pay for each pixel?

No. There is no price per pixel, and the network fee is paid by the project. Holding at least 10,000 $GLOW is the only requirement to draw.

Will my wallet pop up on every click?

No. You connect once and approve a session key that can only claim pixels and expires on its own. After that every click signs itself.

How is my payout worked out?

All trading fees go into the holders' pool. The pool pays out 20% of what it holds per day, in small slices every 10 minutes, split by average live pixels. The rest is a cushion that keeps payouts going when trading slows.

What if someone paints over me?

That pixel stops counting for you the moment it's taken, and your share drops in real time. Take it back, or grow somewhere else.

Can I check a wallet without connecting?

Yes. Paste any address below to see its live pixels, share and estimated payout. Connecting is only needed to draw.

Is this live on-chain?

Yes, on Solana mainnet. Every pixel and every payout is a real transaction you can open in the explorer.
