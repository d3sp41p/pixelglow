Questions, filed

  • Cost

    No. There is no price per pixel. Holding at least the minimum $GLOW is the only requirement to draw.

  • Wallet

    No. You connect once and approve a session key that can only claim pixels and expires on its own. After that every click signs itself.

  • Payouts

    Every 10 minutes, 80% of the epoch's pool is split between holders by their average live pixels over that epoch. The other 20% goes to the treasury for payout gas and upkeep.

  • Overwrites

    That pixel stops counting for you the moment it's taken, and your share drops in real time. Take it back, or grow somewhere else.

  • Read-only

    Yes. Paste any address below to see its live pixels, share and estimated payout. Connecting is only needed to draw.

  • Status

    It runs on Solana devnet with a test $GLOW while the token launches. Every pixel and every payout is a real transaction you can open in the explorer.
